<objective>
Retire the soil tab's two permanently-null KPI slots and put the two SQR-derived numbers in their place, all
the way through to the app's published metadata.

Purpose: D-15 applies Phase 8 D-18 verbatim — `n_surplus_kg_ha` and `p_surplus_kg_ha` are live-confirmed
unavailable at Kreis level on both Destatis platforms (STATE.md), both carry `genesis_table: null` and
`source_host: null`, and both render a permanent em-dash today. D-16 replaces them with the mean SQR and the
rated-area share so the tab reads as three real numbers. D-17 merges them through a new `source_host` branch
in `generate_metadata.py::_build_kpi_by_tab()` — the Phase 05.1 D-03 / Phase 8 D-23 pattern applied a third
time, a new branch rather than a new mechanism, and never a patch into `destatis_ll.json`, which
`aggregate_ll()` destructively regenerates. `14-CONTEXT.md` names the consequence the planner must handle in
the same commit: the locked per-tab KPI counts and the `"soil": 3` assertions in the test suite.

Output: the manifest and its generator updated, a new merge branch, regenerated metadata in both locations,
and the test contract moved in lockstep.
</objective>
