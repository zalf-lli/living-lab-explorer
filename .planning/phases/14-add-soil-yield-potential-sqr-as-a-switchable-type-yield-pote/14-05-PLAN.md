<objective>
Compute the two SQR-derived numbers the soil tab's KPI bar and the PDF reports will show, and write them to
their own committed JSON file.

Purpose: D-16 turns the soil tab from "one real number plus two permanent em-dashes" into three real
numbers. D-14 fixes the primary statistic as the area-weighted mean SQR over rated cells only — it answers
"how good is this region's cropland?", which is the question the dataset exists to answer, and it does not
penalise a good-soil Living Lab for also containing forest. D-16's second tile, the share of Living Lab area
carrying any SQR score, is the honest denominator for that mean and is also what explains the grey areas on
the map. `14-CONTEXT.md` warns explicitly that these two denominators must not be "harmonised": the mean is
over rated cells, the chart in plan 14-07 is over the whole Living Lab, and the second tile is the bridge.

Output: one new pipeline script and one committed `data/sqr_kpis.json`.
</objective>

Create `data-pipeline/python/compute_sqr_kpis.py` adapted from `compute_climate_kpis.py`.

Reuse verbatim, do not re-derive: `METRIC_CRS = "EPSG:25832"`, the reproject-the-whole-raster-to-metric-CRS
**before** masking ordering, and the `gpd.read_file(...)` followed immediately by
`boundaries.geometry.make_valid()`. `14-RESEARCH.md`'s "Don't Hand-Roll" table is explicit that reordering
the reprojection relative to the mask silently reintroduces the latitude-weighting bug that
`08-RESEARCH.md` Pitfall 4 documents. Copy the analog's docstring warning about that ordering into this
script so the next reader sees it here too. Note in a comment that although EPSG:3034 is the source CRS,
this script still reprojects to EPSG:25832 because that is the metric CRS every other zonal-stats script in
this pipeline uses — consistency with `compute_climate_kpis.py` and `compute_protected_area_coverage.py`
matters more than trusting EPSG:3034's own area properties.

Boundaries come from `data/ll_boundaries.geojson` (the true, unbuffered Living Lab boundary), matching
`compute_climate_kpis.py` — not the 2 km-buffered `data/nuts3_ll.geojson` extent the tile build uses. Record
that difference in a comment; the two boundary conventions coexist deliberately in this pipeline and the
split must not be "tidied up".

Two figures per Living Lab, computed in the same masked-array scope so they cannot drift:
  - `sqr_mean_score` — the mean over **rated cells only**, i.e. `np.nanmean` after mapping the `-9999`
    sentinel and every non-finite value to NaN. Round to 1 decimal place. Raise a `RuntimeError` naming the
    slug if zero finite pixels remain after masking (CLAUDE.md's silent-clip-failure rule, the same guard
    the analog already has).
  - `sqr_rated_area_pct` — the count of finite (rated) pixels divided by the count of pixels inside the
    masked Living Lab footprint, times 100, rounded to 1 decimal place. The denominator is the masked
    footprint, not the raster window: `rasterio.mask` with `crop=True` returns a rectangular array, so derive
    the in-boundary pixel count from the mask itself rather than from the array shape. Getting this wrong
    silently deflates every Living Lab's percentage, so assert the result lies in the open interval (0, 100]
    and raise naming the slug otherwise.

Add a plausibility gate before writing, mirroring the analog's own gate: every `sqr_mean_score` must fall
within the declared scale (0 to `yield_bands.scale_max`, read from `sources.yaml`, not hardcoded) and, given
the raster's observed German range of roughly 15 to 99, a mean outside 10-100 indicates a unit or masking
bug rather than a real region. Fail loudly with the offending slug and value.

Write `data/sqr_kpis.json` (the path from `output.kpi_file`, not a literal) with a `_meta` block carrying
`computed_at`, `metric_crs`, `source: "sqr1000"`, `input_path`, `boundary_source`, and a `variables` map
giving both keys their bilingual `unit` — `sqr_mean_score` unit EN "points" / DE "Punkte",
`sqr_rated_area_pct` unit EN "%" / DE "%". Serialize with `json.dumps(..., ensure_ascii=False, indent=2,
sort_keys=True)`.

Close the module with the analog's own discipline comment: this script writes `OUTPUT_FILE` and nothing
else. It must never read or write `data/destatis_ll.json` (which `aggregate_ll()` destructively regenerates
— D-17) and never write `data/ll_content.json` (human-owned — CLAUDE.md).

Give it an argparse surface matching the analog: `--layer` defaulting to `sqr1000`, `--ll` to restrict to a
single slug, and a dry-run or list flag if the analog has one.
