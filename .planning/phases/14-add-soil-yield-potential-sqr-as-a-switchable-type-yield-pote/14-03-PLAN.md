<objective>
Register SQR1000 as a second `sources.yaml` layer joined to `app_layer: soil`, and make that legal — today
it is not, because `sync.py:306` builds `LAYER_SOURCE_INDEX` as a flat last-write-wins map. Declare the
band partition once, here, and codegen the app's yield legend from it.

Purpose: `14-CONTEXT.md` names the `app_layer` 1:1 collision as "the single highest-risk item in the phase,
it fails quietly rather than loudly, and it must be designed before any sources.yaml edit lands".
`14-RESEARCH.md` Pattern 1 confirms the mechanism at the exact cited line and prescribes an additive
compound-key companion map plus a hard build-time assertion. D-22 requires the band partition — used by the
tile palette bake (14-04), the app legend, and the chart script (14-07) — to come from one declaration.

Output: the `sqr1000` entry, a mode-aware `generate_layer_sources()`, a new `generate_soil_yield_legend()`
codegen step, both regenerated JS modules, and a contract test that turns any future collision into a
failing test.
</objective>
