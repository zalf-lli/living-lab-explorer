<objective>
Teach the R report's chart accessors that the soil tab now has two chart JSON files, and teach its bar-colour
resolver about the SQR band palette — without changing what any existing caller resolves.

Purpose: `LL_TAB_CHART_LAYER` is a one-layer-per-tab map, and both `ll_class_area_df()` (which supplies the
bar legend under every thematic map) and `.ll_chart_path()` (which supplies the chart figure) dispatch through
it. The soil tab now has `buek250-{slug}.json` and `sqr1000-{slug}.json`, and D-18 has the report render both
maps, each with its own bar legend. `.ll_bar_color_resolver()`'s `identical(tab, "soil")` branch routes every
soil bar through `ll_soil_color()`'s FNV-1a hash into the BUEK palette, which would paint SQR band bars with
soil-group colours. `14-PATTERNS.md` says the resolver must branch on the series' own shape rather than on
`tab` alone.

Output: three R modules extended additively, plus their unit-test module updated.
</objective>
