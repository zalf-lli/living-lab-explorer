<objective>
Carry the SQR band declaration and the new bilingual strings across the third hop of this project's codegen
chain, so the R report draws the yield map from the same colours and edges the browser does.

Purpose: `14-RESEARCH.md`'s Anti-Patterns section is emphatic that `sources.yaml` is not mechanically "the"
single declaration point reachable by all three D-22 consumers. The real shape is a three-hop chain —
`sources.yaml` to (automatic, `sync.py`) JS module to (manual, developer-run) `export_report_tokens.mjs` to
`data/report_tokens.json` to R — and the middle-to-last hop is the one that is easy to forget because
`python sync.py` does not perform it. `14-PATTERNS.md` Pattern D says the same. This plan is that hop, made
explicit so plan 14-12's R accessors have something to read.

Output: an extended export script, a regenerated committed bridge file, and an updated contract test.
</objective>
