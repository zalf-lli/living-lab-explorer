<objective>
Resolve the one question in Phase 14 that no amount of code can settle: whether this project may
publish SQR1000-derived map tiles on a public static deployment, and exactly which provenance strings
credit the dataset when it does.

Purpose: `14-RESEARCH.md`'s Licence Risk section found — across four independent official BGR-controlled
sources — that SQR1000 is governed by BGR's General Standard Terms and Conditions (GSTC / "Allgemeine
Geschaeftsbedingungen"), **not** GeoNutzV as BUEK250 is, and that GSTC Article 3(1) explicitly withholds
"the right to make accessible to the public" outside an administrative-procedure carve-out that does not
describe this project. D-06 also states in its own words that the licence text "needs research confirmation —
do not assume GeoNutzV by analogy with BUEK250". Every downstream plan writes or renders that string, so it
must be decided first and decided once.

Output: `14-LICENCE.md` carrying the evidence, the options, the human's verdict, and the four verbatim
strings (`provider`, `dataset`, `license`, `attribution`, `citation`) plan 14-03 copies into `sources.yaml`.
</objective>
