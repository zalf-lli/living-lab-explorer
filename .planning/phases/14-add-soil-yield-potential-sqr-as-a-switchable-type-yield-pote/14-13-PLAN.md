<objective>
Put the yield map into the PDF report's soil section as a full-size second figure with its own bar legend,
its own note and its own source credit.

Purpose: D-18 requires it. The PDF is the offline substitute for the app, and a control the reader cannot
operate must become two figures — the climate section already precedents this by rendering all four picker
variables rather than dropping the ones behind the control. An inset-sized second map was explicitly rejected
as unreadable at 250 m. The bar legend must draw its lengths from plan 14-07's committed chart JSON, because
`legend_bars.R`'s header rule forbids recomputing a statistic in R and `.ll_climate_band_shares()` is the one
exception D-19 exists to avoid repeating.

Output: a new R map function with its height helper, an extended soil section in the Quarto template, and R
unit-test coverage.
</objective>
