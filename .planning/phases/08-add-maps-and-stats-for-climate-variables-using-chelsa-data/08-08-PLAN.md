<objective>
Run the two-pass build for real, commit the sixty PMTiles, teach `sync.py` to publish a
three-placeholder pattern, and codegen the legend module the app will read.

Two things here are real code changes rather than data flow-through. `sync_pmtiles_per_ll()` at
`sync.py:164-183` replaces exactly one placeholder — `pattern.replace("{slug}", "*")` — and the
climate pattern carries three, so without a fix the glob matches nothing and the layer silently
never publishes. And `generate_climate_legend()` has no true analog: `generate_land_cover_legend()`
emits a flat categorical array with no notion of mode or unit family, while climate's legend is
per-variable, per-mode, and formats its band labels differently depending on whether the variable
reports an absolute delta or a percent change.

Purpose: this is the plan where the phase's data actually lands on disk and in the app's public
directory. Volume is the standing risk — 60 files against Phase 6's 5 and Phase 3's 1.
Output: 60 committed PMTiles, their published copies, `app/src/data/climate_legend.js`, and two new
functions in `sync.py`.
</objective>
