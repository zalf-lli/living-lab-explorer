<objective>
Complete D-01 on the pipeline side: rename the internal tab join key `landuse` to `agriculture` everywhere it identifies the app tab, and regenerate the derived data so the Agriculture tab keeps its four Destatis KPIs.

Purpose: `LAYERS[].id`, `kpiByTab` keys and `sources.yaml`'s `app_layer` are matched by string. Plan 06-03 renames the layer id in the app; if this side is not renamed in the same phase, `StatPanel` looks up `ll.kpiByTab['agriculture']`, finds nothing, and the Agriculture tab silently loses all four indicators, while `MapInfoControl` loses the crop-types attribution. The rename is a functional coupling, not cosmetics — which is why it is in scope rather than deferred.
Output: The rename applied at its source of truth, the derived JSON artefacts regenerated by the pipeline, and the two hardcoded test assertions updated in the same change.
</objective>
