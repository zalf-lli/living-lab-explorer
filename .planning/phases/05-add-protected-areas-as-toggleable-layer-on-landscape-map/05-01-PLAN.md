  <decision>At what coordinate precision should the protected-areas GeoJSON be committed?</decision>
  <context>
    This is asked first because it is irreversible in practice. Task 3 writes five files into
    `data/` and five identical copies into `app/public/`, both tracked by git, on a repository whose
    `.git` is already about 725 MB. Once committed, changing precision later leaves the larger blobs
    in history forever. Deferring the question to end-of-phase verification would mean asking after
    the cost had already been paid.

    D-08 says "render all polygon features without simplification or downsampling, accepting
    potential interaction slowness on large datasets for data fidelity". All three options below
    satisfy D-08 literally: none removes a single vertex or a single feature. `set_precision` is
    topology-aware coordinate *rounding*, measured across all 1,248 features as keeping 1,248 of
    1,248 at both 1e-6 and 1e-5. The difference is purely how many significant digits per ordinate
    are written to disk. The question is therefore about repository cost, not fidelity to D-08 —
    but the strictest reading of D-08 is still "write exactly what the service returned", which is
    Option A.

    For reference, `build_vector.py` already applies `set_precision(0.0001)` (about 11 m) to the
    BUEK250 soil layer, which is 100x coarser than Option B.
  </context>
  <options>
    <option id="option-a">
      <name>Option A — raw precision, no rounding (strictest D-08 reading)</name>
      <pros>Byte-for-byte what the WFS returned; zero interpretation of D-08 required; no rounding step to justify or explain later.</pros>
      <cons>40.4 MB in `data/` plus 40.4 MB in `app/public/` = about 80.8 MB committed; browser fetch is 2.75 to 12.55 MB per Living Lab uncompressed; roughly 15 significant digits per ordinate, most of which encode sub-micron positions that no zoom level can display.</cons>
    </option>
    <option id="option-b">
      <name>Option B — set_precision(1e-6), about 11 cm rounding (research recommendation)</name>
      <pros>Cuts the committed total to about 47.2 MB, saving roughly 34 MB; verified to keep all 1,248 features and every vertex; visually lossless at every zoom level Leaflet supports; still 100x finer than the precedent already applied to BUEK250.</pros>
      <cons>Requires accepting that rounding is not "downsampling" under D-08 (an interpretation, though one already confirmed); coordinates are no longer byte-identical to the service response.</cons>
    </option>
    <option id="option-c">
      <name>Option C — set_precision(1e-5), about 1.1 m rounding</name>
      <pros>Smallest footprint at about 41.7 MB committed; still verified to keep all 1,248 features and every vertex; 1.1 m is far below the positional accuracy of the source data itself.</pros>
      <cons>Saves only about 5.5 MB more than Option B while being 10x coarser; the extra coarseness buys little and is harder to defend against a strict D-08 reading.</cons>
    </option>
  </options>
  <resume-signal>Select: option-a, option-b, or option-c. State the literal to write into `sources.yaml` as `wfs.coordinate_precision` (`null` for A, `0.000001` for B, `0.00001` for C).</resume-signal>
