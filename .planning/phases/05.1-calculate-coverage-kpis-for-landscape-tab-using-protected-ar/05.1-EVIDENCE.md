### Attribution Question (Step 7)

These two KPI tiles deliberately do **NOT** carry in-panel BfN source attribution, because:
- `StatPanel.jsx` builds its "view source" disclosure from fields with a truthy `genesisTable`
- Both protected-area slots keep `genesisTable: null` (they are not GENESIS tables)
- Therefore, no in-panel "view source" entry is rendered for these two tiles

**Workaround:** BfN attribution is visible on the **protected-areas map overlay's own info control** (added in Phase 5). Toggling the protected-areas layer reveals its legend and attribution immediately.

**Decision recorded at verification checkpoint (Task 2):** Does the absence of in-panel BfN attribution acceptable as shipped, or should it be added as follow-up work?
