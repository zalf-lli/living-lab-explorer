<objective>
Record defensible evidence for the five locked decisions and the ten published figures, then have a
human confirm the Landscape tab actually renders them correctly in both languages.

Purpose: this phase publishes numbers that a researcher or stakeholder may cite. The computation is
several spatial operations deep (dissolve, reproject, clip, area) and its two most dangerous failure
modes — an unclipped sum inflating coverage, and a wrong-CRS area — both produce numbers that look
superficially fine in a JSON file but are obviously wrong once compared against the Living Lab's
total area. This plan makes that comparison explicit and puts a human in front of the rendered
result before the phase closes.

Per **D-01**, the check confirms exactly two new figures appear and no percentage KPI was added.
Per **D-04**, the evidence record captures which Phase 5 output the numbers were derived from, so a
future Phase 5 re-fetch can be traced to a change in these KPIs.

Output: `05.1-EVIDENCE.md` and a human sign-off.
</objective>
