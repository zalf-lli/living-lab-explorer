Start the dev server: `cd app && npm run dev`, then open the printed URL.

**A. Entry (EN)**
1. Go to `#/ll/east-brandenburg` and click "+ Add for comparison".
2. Expect a 220px dropdown headed "Compare with" with exactly four rows — Havellaendisches Luch,
   Hessian Low Mountain, North Hessian Loess, Rheingau — each with an LL icon on the left and a small
   coloured circle on the right. East Brandenburg must NOT be listed.
3. Press Escape — it closes. Reopen, click far away on the page — it closes. Reopen, click inside it —
   it stays open.
4. Click "Rheingau".

**B. The two-column view**
5. Expect two columns: East Brandenburg left, Rheingau right, each topped by a thin coloured bar.
   Confirm the left bar's colour matches the left map's boundary outline, and likewise on the right.
6. Confirm exactly one row of layer tabs, above both columns. Click through Agriculture, Climate,
   Soil, Economic, Landscape — both columns change together every time.
7. Confirm each column has its own legend under its own map. On the Soil tab, the two legends may
   list different soil types — that is correct and intended.
8. Confirm the KPI tiles are 2-across in each column (four KPIs = two rows) and that the two columns'
   rows sit at the same height.
9. Scroll the page. There must be ONE scrollbar moving both columns together, not two.
10. Confirm there is no dashed lime "Add for comparison" card anywhere on this page.
11. Reload the page — the two-column view comes back exactly as it was.

**C. The comparison bar**
12. The A/B "Change layout" switcher is gone; in its place: "Comparing ● East Brandenburg ↔ ●
    Rheingau", a "⇄ Swap sides" button and a "✕ Exit comparison" button. The two dots match the two
    column accent colours.
13. Click "⇄ Swap sides" — the columns exchange places and the URL becomes
    `#/ll/rheingau?compare=east-brandenburg`.
14. Click either LL name — the same "Compare with" dropdown opens. Pick a different LL; the right
    column changes to it.
15. Select the **Soil** tab, then swap sides, then change the partner. Soil must stay selected
    throughout (it must NOT snap back to Landscape).

**D. Header pills**
16. While comparing, click a different Living Lab pill in the site header — that LL becomes the LEFT
    column and the partner stays on the right.
17. Click the pill of the LL currently on the RIGHT — the two swap sides.
18. Confirm the pill of the LL currently in the route is visually highlighted.

**E. Exit and invalid URLs**
19. Click "✕ Exit comparison" — back to the normal single-LL page, with the dashed CTA card visible
    and the same layout (Split or Stacked) you were using before you entered comparison.
20. Paste `#/ll/east-brandenburg?compare=bogus` — the page shows the normal single-LL view and the
    `?compare=bogus` disappears from the address bar with no error message. Repeat with
    `?compare=east-brandenburg` and `?compare=__proto__`; both must behave identically.

**F. German**
21. Switch the language to DE and repeat steps A, B and C once. Expect: dropdown heading "Vergleichen
    mit", bar prefix "Vergleich:", buttons "⇄ Seiten tauschen" and "✕ Vergleich beenden", and the CTA
    "+ Zum Vergleich hinzufuegen". Confirm no German string shows a broken character, and that the
    glyphs ⇄ and ✕ render as symbols rather than boxes.

**G. Coverage across LLs**
22. Try at least three different pairs spanning all five Living Labs (e.g. Havellaendisches Luch ↔
    Rheingau, Hessian Low Mountain ↔ North Hessian Loess, East Brandenburg ↔ Hessian Low Mountain).
    Every pair must render two complete columns with both maps drawing.
23. Throttle the network in DevTools (Slow 3G) and reload a comparison URL: the two maps must load
    independently — whichever finishes first appears first, and the other shows "Loading map..." on
    its own. Neither column may block the other, and no page-wide error banner may appear.
