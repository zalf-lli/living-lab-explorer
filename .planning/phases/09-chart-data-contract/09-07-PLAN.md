  <how-to-verify>
    Read `.planning/phases/09-chart-data-contract/09-EVIDENCE.md` Section 3 first - it has
    every figure in one table. Then open the files under `data/charts/` for anything that
    needs a closer look. Suggested order, roughly cheapest to most subtle:

    1. Agriculture (`data/charts/landuse-croptypes-*.json`): do the dominant crop types match
       what you know about each region? East-brandenburg and havelland are
       Brandenburg arable landscapes; rheingau is a wine region. If rheingau's vineyard share
       is negligible or a Brandenburg Living Lab is dominated by an implausible crop, say so.
    2. Soil (`data/charts/buek250-*.json`): are the leading soil groups plausible per region,
       and is each `label.de` correct, readable German - not a machine-mangled BUEK string?
    3. Landscape (`data/charts/io-lulc-landcover-*.json`): do forest, cropland, grassland and
       settlement shares look right for each region?
    4. Economic (`data/charts/boris-*.json`): the categories are Bodenrichtwert usage types,
       counted as zones rather than area. Is the German wording correct and are the
       abbreviated forms acceptable, for example `gemischte Baufl.` and
       `Baufl. fuer Gemeinbedarf`? If any category should read differently in German, name it.
    5. Climate (`data/charts/chelsa-climate-*.json`): four lines, two points each, all as
       percent change against the 1981-2010 baseline. Growing degree days and mean annual
       temperature should be positive and larger at 2071-2100 than at 2041-2070 under
       SSP3-7.0. Precipitation signs may legitimately differ by region and horizon. Note that
       percent change of a Celsius temperature is a deliberate comparability device (D-09) so
       all four variables share one axis - it is not a physical rate; confirm you are content
       with that framing for the eventual chart.
    6. Across all five: `unit` reads `ha` for agriculture, soil and landscape; `zones`/`Zonen`
       for economic; `%` for climate. Confirm those are the units you want a chart axis
       labelled with.
    7. Climate variable names (`sources.yaml` `climate.variables.{id}.label`): "Growing degree
       days"/"Wachstumsgradtage", "Mean annual temperature"/"Jahresmitteltemperatur", "Annual
       precipitation"/"Jahresniederschlag", "Precipitation of warmest quarter"/"Niederschlag
       im waermsten Quartal". These are deliberately longer than the app's existing
       abbreviated picker labels (GDD, Mean temp., Precipitation, Summer precip.), because a
       chart legend has more room than a button. Confirm both sets should coexist.
