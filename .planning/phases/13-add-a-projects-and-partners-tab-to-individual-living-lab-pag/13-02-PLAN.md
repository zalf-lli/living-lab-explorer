<objective>
Land the three non-visual JavaScript modules the Partners & Projects tab is built on: the shared
boundary helpers extracted out of `LLMap`, a pure node-importable partners/projects logic module,
and the lazy module-cached fetch hook.

Purpose: these are the pieces with real logic and no JSX, so they can be gated by actual `node`
assertions in a project that has no JS test runner (the `app/src/lib/chartSeries.js` precedent from
Phase 11). Extracting `selectBoundary`/`getBounds` before `PartnersMap` exists prevents the
copy-paste duplication 13-RESEARCH.md Pattern 1 names as the anti-pattern to avoid.
Output: two new `lib/` modules, one new hook, one net-zero-behaviour refactor of `LLMap`.
</objective>

    everything else, including `javascript:`, `data:`, `file:`, relative paths and unparseable
    strings. This is the runtime mitigation for threat T-13-01; every `<a href>` and every
    `window.open` call in plans 13-03/13-04 routes through it.
