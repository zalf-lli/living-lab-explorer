  <what-built>
The Socio-economic tab now renders a BORIS standard-land-value choropleth for every Living Lab: 63,735 zone
polygons across the five regions, fetched from the Brandenburg and Hessen BORIS WFS services at pipeline
time and committed as static GeoJSON. Fill colour encodes euro-per-square-metre through six per-Living-Lab
quantile buckets on a teal-to-orange ramp; zones with no current valuation render in a muted grey with a
dashed border; the legend lists each bucket's exact euro range plus a note that the scale is relative to
that Living Lab only; hovering a zone shows its value, bilingual usage type, and valuation date. Rendering
goes through a Canvas layer because the two Brandenburg Living Labs carry 19,083 and 30,018 zones. The
existing Socio-economic StatPanel with its Destatis KPIs is unchanged and renders alongside the map.
  </what-built>

For **each** of the five Living Labs (Rheingau, North Hessian Loess, Hessian Low Mountain, Havellaendisches
Luch, East Brandenburg), open the LL detail page and select the **Socio-economic** tab. Then repeat the whole
pass with the language switcher set to **Deutsch**.

1. **Map renders.** Zone polygons appear and fill the region. The two Brandenburg Living Labs are the heavy
   ones — note whether panning and zooming stay responsive, and whether hovering feels laggy.

   visually varied rather than one flat colour. If a Living Lab looks almost entirely one colour, say so.
3. **Legend.** Six colour swatches, each labelled with an actual euro range such as "4-9 €/m²" — not
   "low"/"high" and not a bare gradient. A seventh grey "No current value" swatch appears only where such
   zones exist. Below the legend, the note stating the scale applies to this Living Lab only appears, in the
   selected language.
4. **No-data zones.** Grey dashed zones, where present, are clearly distinguishable from every ramp colour
   and do not read as "cheapest".
5. **Tooltip.** Hover several zones. Exactly three pieces of information: the value with its unit, the usage
   type, and the valuation date. The usage type must be in the selected language (English usage labels in
   EN, German in DE). There must be **no** zone reference number row and **no** development-status row. On a
   grey no-data zone, the tooltip must show "No current value" / "Kein aktueller Wert" and must not display
